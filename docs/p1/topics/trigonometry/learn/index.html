Skill 1 of 8

Read amplitude, period and asymptotes from trigonometric formulae

Read amplitude, period and asymptote information from a trigonometric formula.

Need to know

  • Amplitude applies to sine and cosine.
  • Tangent has asymptotes and period π\piπ.

Common error: Giving tangent a maximum and minimum like sine.

Worked example

State the amplitude and period of y=3sin⁡2xy=3\sin 2xy=3sin2x.

  1. The outside factor gives amplitude 3.
  2. The factor 2 inside gives period 2π/2=π2\pi/2=\pi2π/2=π.

Answer: Amplitude 3, period π\piπ

Skill 7 of 8

Construct a trigonometric identity proof

Write a valid chain of identity transformations.

Need to know

  • Work from one side only until it matches the other.
  • Name or visibly apply each basic identity without assuming the conclusion.

Common error: Changing both sides at once or cancelling terms across addition.

Worked example

Prove (1−cos⁡2x)/sin⁡x=sin⁡x(1-\cos^2x)/\sin x=\sin x(1−cos2x)/sinx=sinx where defined.

  1. Use 1−cos⁡2x=sin⁡2x1-\cos^2x=\sin^2x1−cos2x=sin2x.
  2. Then sin⁡2x/sin⁡x=sin⁡x\sin^2x/\sin x=\sin xsin2x/sinx=sinx where sin⁡x≠0\sin x\ne0sinx=0.

Answer: The left side simplifies to the right side
