Skill 1 of 5

Sketch and use sine, cosine and tangent graphs

Sketch transformed trigonometric graphs with correct key features.

Need to know

  • Amplitude applies to sine and cosine.
  • Tangent has asymptotes and period π\piπ.

Common error: Giving tangent a maximum and minimum like sine.

Worked example

State the amplitude and period of y=3sin⁡2xy=3\sin 2xy=3sin2x.

  1. The outside factor gives amplitude 3.
  2. The factor 2 inside gives period 2π/2=π2\pi/2=\pi2π/2=π.

Answer: Amplitude 3, period π\piπ
