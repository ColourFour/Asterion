Skill 3 of 3

Combine sectors, triangles and segments

Assemble a composite perimeter or area from simple pieces.

Need to know

  • Label every arc, radius, triangle and sector contribution.
  • Use sector minus triangle for a minor segment.

Common error: Counting only the curved edge in a perimeter.

Worked example

How is a minor segment area found from its sector and triangle?

  1. Find the sector area.
  2. Subtract the triangle formed by the two radii and chord.

Answer: Sector area minus triangle area
