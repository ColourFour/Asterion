Skill 3 of 8

Recognise arithmetic and geometric progressions

Identify a progression by its invariant difference or ratio.

Need to know

  • Arithmetic progressions have constant difference.
  • Geometric progressions have constant non-zero ratio.

Common error: Calling a sequence geometric because its differences change regularly.

Worked example

Classify 3,6,12,24,…3,6,12,24,\ldots3,6,12,24,….

  1. Each term is multiplied by 2.
  2. The common ratio is 2.

Answer: Geometric progression
