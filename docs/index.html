The Asterion Learning Loop

A repeatable system that builds exam-ready mathematical behavior.

  1. 1 Try the problem

    You attempt first. Your attempt is the center.

  2. 2 Compare your first move

    Asterion compares your move, not just your final answer.

  3. 3 Learn the method

    Explanation appears only when you are ready for it.

  4. 4 Pass a Skill Check

    Deterministic checks prove the small skill before you proceed.

  5. 5 Train on real exam questions

    Work on real CAIE Paper 3 questions and get reviewed.

  6. 6 Review and repair

    Mistakes are expected, repaired, and tracked.
