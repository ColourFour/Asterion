About Asterion

Asterion teaches the behavior behind exam-facing mathematics.

Students attempt first, compare their route, repair the gap, and then train on real CAIE questions. The system is deliberately evidence-first.

The Asterion Learning Loop

A repeatable system that builds exam-facing mathematical behavior.

  1. 1 Try the problem

    You attempt first. Your attempt is the center.

  2. 2 Compare your first move

    Asterion compares your move, not just your final answer.

  3. 3 Learn the method

    Explanation appears after your first attempt.

  4. 4 Complete Checked Practice

    A clean Checked Practice pass is the strongest local evidence.

  5. 5 Train on real exam questions

    Exam Training is self-marked practice. It helps you prepare, but it does not replace Checked Practice evidence unless your teacher says so.

  6. 6 Review and repair

    Mistakes are expected, repaired, and tracked.
