# P1 Student Trial Observation Guide - 2026-06-03

## Trial Goal

Run a small, low-stakes student trial of the P1 draft support layer to check whether students can use the dashboard, Field Guides, and Skill Checks without misunderstanding the content as reviewed mastery, readiness, marks, or final assessment evidence.

The trial is not intended to prove syllabus completeness or assessment readiness. It is intended to reveal wording, navigation, answer-shape, setup, and teacher-support issues before a broader P1 pass.

## Recommended Student Instructions

Tell students before they start:

- This is draft P1 practice support.
- Saved attempts are not marks, mastery, readiness evidence, teacher evidence, or final assessment evidence.
- Use the Field Guide before attempting the Skill Checks.
- Write working on paper for multiple-choice questions, especially where a complete solution set or setup is required.
- If a question feels outside what has been taught, flag it rather than guessing silently.
- Integration sections labelled teacher-guided draft should be attempted only with teacher guidance.

Suggested opening script:

> Today you are testing a draft P1 practice layer. Your saved attempts help us see whether the practice is clear, but they do not count as marks, mastery, readiness, or final assessment evidence. Start with the Field Guide for your unit, then try a few Skill Checks. Write down anything confusing.

## Units To Test First

Prioritize a balanced sample rather than trying to finish the whole course:

1. Quadratics - baseline usability check because this is the strongest draft template.
2. Trigonometry - watch complete solution-set reasoning and identity scope.
3. Circular Measure - watch compound setup and perimeter/area confusion.
4. Differentiation - watch rates-of-change interpretation.
5. Integration - teacher-guided only, focused on setup and scope warnings.

If time allows, add one lighter sample from Functions and Transformations, Series, Binomial Expansion, or Coordinate Geometry to check whether the thinner units feel navigable.

## First 10-15 Minutes

Recommended flow:

1. Students open the P1 dashboard and choose one assigned unit.
2. Students read the unit overview and the first relevant Field Guide section.
3. Students attempt 2-4 Skill Checks from that unit.
4. Students save at most one support-only attempt so the teacher can see whether the warning is understood.
5. Students note one thing that helped and one thing that confused them.

The teacher should avoid reteaching immediately unless the student is blocked by wording, route confusion, answer-entry confusion, or an Integration teacher-guided draft section.

## What The Teacher Should Watch For

- Students skipping Field Guides and going straight to hints.
- Students treating the saved attempt button as a grade, mark, mastery score, readiness signal, or required completion.
- Students answering multiple-choice items by pattern recognition without written working.
- Students confusing exact-answer entry with decimal entry, TeX entry, or hidden-code style answers.
- Students not noticing the student trial warning on practice pages.
- Students not noticing teacher-guided draft wording in Integration.
- Students getting correct short answers but being unable to explain the setup.
- Students stopping because a Field Guide section is too thin for the related Skill Check.
- Students assuming the P1 draft pages replace exam-bank image practice.

## Confusion Signals

Treat these as useful evidence:

- "Does this count?"
- "Is this my mark?"
- "Did I finish the unit?"
- "Why did it save if it does not count?"
- "Do I type the whole answer or only the number?"
- "Do I include degrees/radians/units?"
- "Where did the formula come from?"
- "This was not in the Field Guide."
- "Is this in P1?"
- "I guessed because it was multiple choice."

## Specific Topic Risks

### Trigonometry Reasoning

Watch whether students can explain reference angle, quadrant, range, and period. A correct multiple-choice solution set without working is not enough evidence that the reasoning is clear.

### Circular Measure Compound Setups

Watch for students mixing arc length, sector area, and sector perimeter. In particular, note whether they remember that perimeter may include two radii as well as the arc.

### Differentiation Rates Of Change

Watch whether students attach meaning to the derivative. The main signal is whether they can say what is changing with respect to what, not only compute a number.

### Integration Setup And Scope

Use Integration as teacher-guided draft support only. Watch whether students understand area setup as top-minus-bottom over the correct interval. For improper integrals and volumes of revolution, watch whether the labels prevent students from treating them as reviewed assessment-ready P1 content.

### Saved Attempts As Progress Or Mastery

Ask at least two students what saving an attempt means. If they say it is a mark, progress score, readiness evidence, mastery evidence, or teacher assessment, the warning copy and classroom instruction need another pass.

Severity guide:

- Blocker: student cannot proceed, route/page is broken, answer is wrong, or wording causes a serious false belief.
- High: several students misunderstand the same important concept, warning, or answer shape.
- Medium: one or two students need support, but the page is usable.
- Low: wording polish, isolated preference, or issue unlikely to affect learning.

## Post-Trial Triage Rules

### Fix Before Next Class

Use this category for:

- Broken routes or missing pages.
- Wrong answers or invalid options.
- P1 pages using wrong course wording.
- Warning copy that students clearly miss or misunderstand.
- Any item that teaches a false method or false syllabus expectation.

### Improve Later

Use this category for:

- Thin Field Guide explanations.
- Items that are correct but too shallow.
- More structured exam-style practice needs.
- Better distractors or richer worked routes.
- Repeated answer-entry hesitation that does not block use.

### Ignore/Noise

Use this category for:

- One-off preference comments.
- Confusion caused by not reading visible instructions.
- Issues caused by classroom pacing rather than page wording.
- Requests for broad new content outside the trial scope.

### Needs Source-Contract Review

Use this category for:

- Any uncertainty about whether a subtopic belongs in P1.
- Integration improper integrals.
- Integration volumes of revolution.
- Trigonometry identity scope if reciprocal identities feel off-map.
- Functions inverse domain/range restrictions.
- Coordinate Geometry circle completing-square scope.
