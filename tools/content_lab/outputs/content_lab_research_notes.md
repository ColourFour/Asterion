## Content Implications

- Keep the P3 anchor topics aligned to syllabus sections 3.1 to 3.9: algebra, logarithmic/exponential functions, trigonometry, differentiation, integration, numerical solution of equations, vectors, differential equations, and complex numbers.
- Prioritize method choice and restrictions over long worked examples. P3 students often lose marks by applying a valid technique to the wrong form, skipping domain checks, losing interval discipline, or using rounded values too early.
- Algebra support should distinguish binomial form, partial-fraction denominator structure, modulus case splitting, polynomial theorem use, and validity restrictions.
- Logarithm support should emphasize domain checks, inverse use of `ln` and `e`, legal log laws, and the straight-line structure behind linearization.
- Trigonometry support should emphasize identity selection, reciprocal relationships, double-angle choice, interval checking, R-form structure, and lost-solution traps.
- Generated warm-ups should remain short, deterministic, and original. They should prepare the first move, a complete move, and a Guardian-style move without resembling full CAIE questions.
