## Quarantined Integration Recommendation

Recommended action: keep hidden.

Do not delete the improper-integral or volume-of-revolution authored items, because the official 2026-2027 P1 syllabus includes both areas. Do not move them into default or optional student-facing Skill Checks yet, because the local Field Guide does not currently teach or sequence those phases.

Restore these items only after adding reviewed Field Guide support, ideally as separate small groups:

- `p1-integration-improper-integrals`: 3-question first check focused on limit setup, convergence condition, and one simple evaluation.
- `p1-integration-volumes-revolution`: 3-question first check focused on choosing the correct volume formula and a simple x-axis or y-axis rotation.

Even after restoration, keep them support-only until P1 source evidence and readiness rules are separately reviewed. Do not use official-syllabus inclusion alone as mastery eligibility.
